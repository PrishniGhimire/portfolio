How I work

My design process

I follow a human-centered approach — every decision is grounded in research, not assumptions.

🔍
Step 01
Discover

User interviews, competitive analysis, and heuristic evaluations to understand the real problem space.

💡
Step 02
Define

Synthesizing insights into clear problem statements, personas, and journey maps that guide every decision.

✏️
Step 03
Design

Sketches → wireframes → high-fidelity prototypes in Figma, with rapid iteration based on stakeholder feedback.

🚀
Step 04
Deliver

Developer handoff with annotated specs, design tokens, and continued collaboration through launch and beyond.

About me

Designer, researcher,
problem-solver

Hi! I'm Prishni Ghimire, a UI/UX designer based in Kathmandu, Nepal. I care deeply about making digital products that are not only beautiful, but genuinely usable by real people.

My background combines design thinking with a strong understanding of user psychology — I believe the best interfaces are the ones users never have to think about.

When I'm not pushing pixels, you'll find me hiking around the Kathmandu Valley, sketching in my notebook, or exploring local art exhibitions.

I also write — words matter

Before I was designing interfaces, I was writing. I worked as a freelance content writer for over a year, crafting articles across a range of topics — from lifestyle and culture to research-driven pieces.

Writing made me a better designer. It taught me to think clearly, communicate purposefully, and understand what people actually want to read — and use. Good design and good writing share the same goal: make it effortless for the reader.
