How I work

My design process

A human-centered approach — every decision grounded in research, not assumptions.

STEP 01

Discover

User interviews, competitive analysis, and heuristic evaluations to understand the real problem space.

STEP 02

Define

Synthesizing insights into clear problem statements, personas, and journey maps that guide every decision.

STEP 03

Design

Sketches to wireframes to high-fidelity prototypes in Figma, iterating quickly on stakeholder feedback.

STEP 04

Deliver

Developer handoff with annotated specs and design tokens, plus continued collaboration through launch.

About me

Designer, researcher, problem-solver

Hi! I'm Prishni Ghimire, a UI/UX designer and digital marketer based in Kathmandu, Nepal. I care about making digital products that are not only beautiful, but genuinely usable by real people.

With a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Information Technology, I understand both sides of the handoff: I design in Figma, build with HTML, CSS and React, and have worked with developers on real client projects. That means my designs are practical to implement.

Alongside design, I manage social media, plan content and ad campaigns, and write SEO-friendly articles — so I think about how a product looks, how it reads, and how people find it.

Away from the screen, you'll find me hiking around the Kathmandu Valley, sketching in my notebook, or exploring local art exhibitions.
